Kingside Privacy Policy

Ironframe Apps · Effective October 31, 2026

The short version

Kingside has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells or shares your data. Games against the computer stay entirely on your device. Online games with a friend store only what's needed to play them: a random player ID, the display name you choose, and the moves of that game.

Playing the computer

Nothing leaves your device. Your settings (sound, board and piece options) are saved in the app's local storage on your device.

Playing a friend online

Online play runs on Supabase, which hosts Kingside's game server. When you start or join an online game, Kingside stores:

  • An anonymous player ID. A random ID created the first time you play online. It isn't tied to your name, email, phone number or any account. There is no sign-up.
  • Your display name. The name you type (up to 24 characters), shown to the person you're playing. You don't have to use your real name.
  • The game itself. A 5-character game code and the list of moves, so both players see the same board.

This data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and is used only to run your games. Only the two players in a game can read or change it.

Other connections

To draw the board and text, Kingside loads the three.js library from jsDelivr and fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web request, those services see your IP address. Kingside sends them no personal data.

Deleting your data

To delete your settings, clear the app's data or uninstall it. To delete your online player ID, display name and games from the server, email vjpisme1234@gmail.com with your display name or a game code, and they will be deleted.

Children

Kingside is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes
